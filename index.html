Empowering the Next Generation of Composers

Original music. Fresh voices. New work across musical traditions.

The Ode To Joy Foundation supports young musicians ready to take the bold step from performance to creation. Through financial support, artistic collaboration, and documentary filmmaking, we help emerging composers develop original music across a range of musical traditions—from chamber music and opera to jazz and other forms of musical expression.

Along the way, we document the creative journey: the first ideas, conversations, experiments, rehearsals, revisions, and performances through which new music comes into being. These compositions and the stories behind them become part of a growing public archive, preserving the work of a new generation of musical creators and sharing it with audiences everywhere.

From Performance to Creation

Many young musicians spend years learning to perform the music of others without being encouraged to create music of their own. The Ode To Joy Foundation invites them to take that next courageous step—to compose.

We believe that every generation should have the opportunity not only to inherit great music, but to add its own voices to our shared cultural life. By supporting young composers and documenting their creative journeys, we help ensure that those voices can be heard, shared, and preserved.

Praise for Following the Ninth

Founder Kerry Candaele's documentary Following the Ninth traces Beethoven's final symphony across several countries and into the lives of people who have found in it expressions of freedom, justice, and joy. It is the first film of the Beethoven/Hero trilogy—and the work from which this Foundation grew.

“Each anecdote builds upon the next to create that rarest of films: a documentary as ineffable and transformative as it sets out to be.”

The Village Voice

“The film is beautiful and powerful. Its affirming message could help address destructive dynamics.”

Bill Moyers
